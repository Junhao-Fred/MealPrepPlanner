Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, inside the app's private storage. MealPrep Planner has no account and no server, and it does not sync between devices. Your data is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the device.

How do I edit or delete an ingredient or a meal?
  • Swipe a pantry item or a planned meal to the right to edit it.
  • Swipe it to the left to delete it. You will be asked to confirm first.
  • You can also delete a meal from its details screen.

Meals already marked as prepared can't be edited until you mark them as not prepared.

What happens when I mark a meal as prepared?

The recipe's ingredients are taken out of your pantry, starting with the stock that expires first. Expired stock is skipped. If the pantry didn't have enough, the app tells you what was missing.

Made a mistake? Open the meal and tap Mark as Not Prepared. You can choose to add the ingredients back to the pantry or leave the pantry as it is.

How is the shopping list calculated?

It adds up the ingredients of every unprepared meal from today to six days ahead, then subtracts the pantry stock that hasn't expired. Only what is still missing is listed.

After shopping, tap the check mark next to an item to add it to your pantry. It then disappears from the list.

Why isn't an ingredient matched with my pantry?

Names are matched without regard to capital letters, spaces or simple plurals, so "Tomato" and "tomatoes" are the same. Units are converted within the same kind:

  • Weight: mg, g, kg, oz, lb
  • Volume: ml, cl, dl, l, tsp, tbsp, cup
  • Count: piece, pcs, count, each, item

Other units, such as "bag" or "can", only match the same unit. Use the same unit in the recipe and the pantry for these.

Why are several batches shown as one pantry item?

Batches of the same ingredient are combined into one item with a total, and each batch's amount and expiry date is listed underneath. When you use some, the batch that expires first is used first. To change a single batch, swipe right and choose which batch to edit.

I'm not getting expiry reminders

Reminders are sent at 9:00 the day before an item expires, or on the day itself if that time has already passed. They only apply to items with an expiry date.

If you declined notifications, turn them on in Settings › Notifications › MealPrep Planner.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for MealPrep Planner and choose a size. The widget shows your next meal and how many ingredients to use soon.

How do I move my data to a new phone, or delete it?

Restore the new device from a backup of the old one, and your meals and pantry come with it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.
