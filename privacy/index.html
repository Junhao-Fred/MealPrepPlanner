Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device

Effective 25 October 2026 · Applies to MealPrep Planner for iPhone and iPad, including its Home Screen widget

In short: MealPrep Planner does not collect, store on a server, share, sell or track any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account and does not connect to the internet, so nothing you enter is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Meal plans: meal names, meal types, dates and times, and whether each meal was prepared.
  • Recipes: ingredient names, quantities and units.
  • Pantry stock: ingredient names, quantities, units and optional expiry dates.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. A short summary of upcoming meals and soon-to-expire ingredients is also kept in a storage area shared only with the app's own widget on the same device, so the widget can show it. It never leaves the device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders before pantry items expire. These are created and delivered entirely on your device; no notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Notifications › MealPrep Planner.

Third parties and tracking

  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we never receive any.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual meals and pantry items in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device. Copies may remain in your own device backups (iCloud or computer), which are managed by you and Apple, not by us.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
